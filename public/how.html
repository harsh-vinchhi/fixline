Steps for each role

Residents

Create an account, report an issue, and follow it under My issues. Rate it once it is resolved.

Staff

Log in with the account the manager created for you. My jobs lists only what is assigned to you, urgent first. Mark each one started or resolved.

Managers

Log in, create staff accounts, assign each issue to a staff member with a date, and watch the overdue and recurring alerts.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

Residents create their own account with name, email and flat number. Staff accounts are created by the manager.

Who can see my photo?

Your photo is stored on the Fixline server. Only you, the manager and the assigned staff member can see it.

What if a neighbour already reported the same problem?

Fixline shows the existing issue. Add yourself so the manager sees how many people are affected.

What if nobody responds in time?

The issue is marked Overdue on My issues and in the Manager dashboard.

Can I report an emergency here?

No. For fire, gas leaks or medical emergencies, call your local emergency number first.

Why is the data gone on another device?

It is saved in the Fixline database, so you can log in from any device and see it. A real version would store it on a server.
