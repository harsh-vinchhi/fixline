How Fixline works

1. Report

Describe it, pick the location and urgency, add a photo.

2. Acknowledge

Every issue gets a number and a response deadline.

3. Assign

A named person owns it, with an expected date.

4. Fix and rate

Staff close the job and the resident rates the result.
