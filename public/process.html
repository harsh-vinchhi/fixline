    In apartment communities, problems are reported through WhatsApp, calls, security staff and face-to-face talks. There is no single flow for capturing, prioritizing, assigning and tracking a problem.

    Goals: quick acknowledgement, know who handles it, know when it will be fixed, avoid repeated calls. Frustrations: complaints vanish in WhatsApp groups, he repeats himself, urgent and minor issues look the same.

  2. Define

    Insight: the communication process is fragmented, not merely the complaint form.

    Apartment residents struggle to get maintenance issues resolved efficiently because complaints are reported through fragmented channels, making it difficult to track requests, identify responsibility, and receive timely updates.

    How might we make maintenance issues easier to report, easier to prioritize, and easier for residents to follow from report to resolution?

  3. Ideate

    Each pain point from the research became a feature.

    Pain pointFeature in Fixline
    Fragmented reportingOne report form for every issue
    Duplicate complaintsDuplicate check with an "Add me" option
    Weak prioritizationThree priority levels with plain guidance
    Missing ownershipNamed owner on every issue
    No status visibilityFive-step progress tracker and expected date
    Missing details for staffPhoto upload and exact location
    No closure or trustResident rating after every fix
    Staff juggling many sourcesStaff page with jobs sorted by priority
    No usable historyHistory log and recurring-problem alert
